# Week 2 on the pro route

**No helpers, one real constraint.** The constraint: **the corpus must be your own**, at least 40
real documents you have the right to use, with 30 labelled queries of your own in the same
format as `eval/retrieval-queries.jsonl`, **and** hybrid search (reciprocal rank fusion of the
lexical and semantic rankings) with the metric it moved, in the reflection.
