# Fabrikam Logistics - Fleet report, first half 2026

## Summary

The fleet grew from 412 to 438 vehicles in the half. Electric vehicles rose from 31 to 57, which
is 13 percent of the fleet. Fuel spend fell 6 percent against the same half last year despite
the larger fleet, mostly from the Felixstowe to Birmingham corridor moving to electric tractors.

## Utilisation

| Depot | Vehicles | Utilisation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Felixstowe | 168 | 84 percent | port congestion in May |
| Birmingham | 121 | 79 percent | new cross-dock opened 3 March |
| Leeds | 88 | 71 percent | driver shortage |
| Glasgow | 61 | 68 percent | seasonal |

## Incidents

There were 14 reportable incidents, down from 19. None involved injury. The most common cause
was reversing damage in tight urban yards; the Leeds depot accounted for 6 of the 14.

## Outlook

The electric tractor order of 40 units from Volta is due in October 2026. Charging capacity at
Birmingham is the constraint: 12 chargers today, 30 needed.
