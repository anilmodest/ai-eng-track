SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 August 2026 between Fabrikam Logistics Inc.
("Client") and Tailspin Analytics Ltd ("Provider").

1. Services. Provider will design, build and operate a document classification service for
Client's freight paperwork, as described in Schedule A.

2. Term. The Agreement begins on 1 September 2026 and runs for twelve (12) months. Either party
may terminate with sixty (60) days written notice.

3. Fees. Client will pay Provider 6,500 GBP per month, invoiced monthly in advance, payable
within 14 days of invoice.

4. Service levels. The service will be available 99.5 percent of each calendar month, measured
at the Provider's API gateway. Credits of 5 percent of the monthly fee apply for each full
percentage point below target.

5. Data. All documents remain Client's property. Provider will not use Client documents to train
any model and will delete them within 30 days of termination.

6. Liability. Provider's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the
preceding six (6) months.

7. Governing law. England and Wales.

Signed for Fabrikam Logistics Inc.: R. Okafor, Chief Operating Officer
Signed for Tailspin Analytics Ltd: M. Lindqvist, Managing Director
