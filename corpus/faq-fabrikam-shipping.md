# Fabrikam Logistics - Customer FAQ

## How do I book a collection?

Use the customer portal or call 0330 123 4567 before 15:00 for next-day collection within
Great Britain. Collections booked after 15:00 are scheduled for the following working day.

## What are your cut-off times for the Felixstowe port?

Export documents must reach us by 12:00 on the working day before the vessel's cut-off. Late
documents can sometimes be accepted for a 75 GBP expedite fee, but this is not guaranteed.

## How is a shipment priced?

By chargeable weight: the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight, where volumetric weight
is length x width x height in centimetres divided by 5,000.

## What is covered if goods are damaged?

Standard liability is 1.30 GBP per kilogram under RHA Conditions of Carriage. Enhanced cover up
to the declared value can be added at booking for 0.5 percent of that value.

## Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Every consignment has a 12-character reference beginning FL. Enter it on the portal or send
it by SMS to 60777 for the latest scan.

## Who do I contact about an invoice?

Email accounts@fabrikam.example with the invoice number. Queries raised within 14 days of the
invoice date are resolved before payment is due.
