# Contoso Pharmaceuticals - Travel and expenses policy (v4, effective 1 July 2026)

## Principles

Spend the company's money as carefully as your own. Book early, travel standard class, and keep
receipts for anything over 10 GBP.

## Rail and air

- Rail within Great Britain: standard class. First class only when it is cheaper on the day.
- Flights under 4 hours: economy. Longer flights: premium economy with line manager approval.
- Book through the travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only with a reason.

## Accommodation

Hotel nightly caps: London 180 GBP, other UK cities 130 GBP, elsewhere per the portal's local cap.
Breakfast is claimable when not included.

## Meals and subsistence

Day rate for meals when away overnight: 40 GBP. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment
needs pre-approval from a director and a named client attendee list.

## Mileage

Personal car: 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in the tax year, then 25 pence.
Electric cars: the same rate. Parking and tolls at cost.

## Claims

Submit within 30 days of the expense through the expenses app. Claims over 500 GBP in a month
route to the finance business partner. Late claims may be refused.
