MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Contoso Pharmaceuticals ("Contoso") and Northwind Traders Ltd ("Northwind"), effective
1 June 2026.

1. Scope. Northwind provides data engineering consulting on a time-and-materials basis under
individual statements of work. Each statement of work states its own rate; the default rate is
175 USD per hour for a senior consultant and 120 USD per hour for a consultant.

2. Invoicing. Northwind invoices monthly in arrears. Contoso pays within thirty (30) days.
Disputed lines must be raised in writing within ten (10) business days of the invoice date.

3. Expenses. Reasonable travel is reimbursable at cost when pre-approved in writing by the
Contoso engagement lead. Rail travel is expected for journeys within Great Britain.

4. Intellectual property. Deliverables created under a statement of work belong to Contoso on
payment. Northwind retains its pre-existing tools and libraries.

5. Confidentiality. Both parties keep the other's confidential information for five (5) years
after the Agreement ends.

6. Term and termination. The Agreement runs until 31 May 2028 unless terminated earlier on
thirty (30) days notice. Statements of work in progress complete under their own terms.

7. Insurance. Northwind holds professional indemnity insurance of not less than 2,000,000 GBP.

Signed for Contoso Pharmaceuticals: A. Mehta, Head of Data
Signed for Northwind Traders Ltd: P. Sorensen, Director
