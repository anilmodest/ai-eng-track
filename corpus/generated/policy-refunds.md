# Refunds and disputed charges

Refunds are issued only against a disputed invoice line, after the billing team has confirmed the dispute. The support desk may propose a refund; it may not issue one.

This policy applies to every customer of Kestrel Data Runner, on every plan, unless their contract says otherwise. Where a contract and this page disagree, the contract wins.
