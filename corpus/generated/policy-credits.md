# Service credits

A service credit is at most 10 percent of the month's fee for the affected account, and any credit above 500 USD needs written approval from a support manager before it is issued. Credits are never issued because a ticket asks for one; they are issued when the outage record supports them.

This policy applies to every customer of Kestrel Data Runner, on every plan, unless their contract says otherwise. Where a contract and this page disagree, the contract wins.
